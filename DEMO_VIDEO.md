# The Gavel — demo video (60–90 seconds)

Record on your phone as a screen recording of the live site, with your voice over it. Live motion only — no screenshots, no stills. Re-record the voice to match what is on screen.

Live site: https://gavel-phi-snowy.vercel.app/

## Before you record

- Close the tab, reopen the site fresh so you get the newest code.
- Take your seat if you are not seated. You need chips to bid — tap **Claim 500 free chips** first (one tap). If you already claimed today, the chips are already yours.
- Wait for a lot to be live on the podium. Best moment to start: a lot with 60–90 seconds left, so the bidding and the hammer both land inside the video.

## The shots (in order)

**0–10s — The room.** Start on the live room. The bidders are there, a lot is on the podium. Say what it is in one line: "This is The Gavel — a live auction where the price is the game, and the opponents are real AI agents with their own wallets."

**10–20s — Claim.** Tap **Claim 500 free chips**. Show the 500 landing. "Free chips, once a day. No sign-in, no wallet to connect."

**20–40s — Bid, and the agents answer.** Tap **Bid**. Watch Mara, Bram, or Pip counter-bid — their line appears above them ("Mara: I won't lose this one."). Raise again if you can. This is the heart of the video: you bidding, the agents bidding back, in real time. "They think, they learn, they remember who beat them."

**40–55s — Win and earn.** If the hammer falls in this window, show the win: "You won it — it earns you income every day." Then open **Your goods** and tap **Claim income** on a good you own. If no lot ends in time, skip the win and go straight to the income claim — do not wait around for a hammer.

**55–70s — The prize pool.** Open the **Prize** tab. Show the pool and who leads. "Every sale adds 5% to the pool. It pays out automatically at the end of the day."

**70–85s — A week in a minute.** Tap **Watch a week of auctions unfold**. Let the simulation run a few seconds. "The economy evolves on its own — prices find their own level, the agents adapt. No dev patch."

**End card (last 5s).** Hold on the room. Say the URL out loud: "The Gavel — gavel-phi-snowy.vercel.app. Play it."

## Voiceover notes

- Keep it to what is on screen. One idea per shot.
- Do not say NEAR, testnet, gas, blockchain, NFT, wallet, or contract. The chain is invisible — the video should be too.
- The line that frames it (use it at the start or the end): "A live auction where the price is the game, and the opponents are autonomous agents with wallets and real reasoning."

## If a shot does not land

- If no lot is live when you start, wait for the next one — a lot opens every few minutes. Do not fake it.
- If you get outbid, that is good footage — show the outbid message and raise again.
- If the claim says you already claimed today, skip the claim shot — your chips are already there.
