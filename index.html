How it works

30 seconds

Claim your free daily chips, pick a lot on the podium, and raise your paddle. Beat the current bid by at least 25 coins. A bid in the last 30 seconds adds 30 seconds, so no one can snipe a lot at the last moment.

5 minutes

Win a lot and the good is yours. It starts earning coins every day straight away. The more a good earns each day, the more it is worth — a good earning 250 a day pays for itself far faster than one earning 60.

A full session

Own goods, buy shares in other people's goods, re-list your goods at a fixed price or put them back up for auction, and come back tomorrow to claim the income that has built up. Every sale adds a little extra to the prize pool — and each day the pool pays out to the three owners whose goods earn the most. See the Prize tab for who leads and when it pays.

Why a lot is worth what it is

Every lot shows its daily income, its last sold price, and who has owned it before. Divide the price by the daily income and you get the payback: how many days of earnings it takes for the good to pay for itself. A low payback and a strong earnings history mean a good deal. A high payback means you are paying for the name, not the earnings.

The three regulars

Mara bids early and bids big — she wants the lot and does not hide it. Bram is patient: he watches, then raises in steady steps when the room goes quiet. Pip has the smallest purse, so every bid is a real decision; when Pip raises, Pip means it.
